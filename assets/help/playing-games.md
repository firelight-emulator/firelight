# Playing games

Select a game in your [Library](/library) to launch it. This page covers what you can do once a
game is running.

## The in-game menu

Bring up the quick menu while playing to pause, adjust settings, manage saves, and exit back to
your library. The menu is also reachable from the sidebar while a game is running.

## Save states & suspend points

Firelight can snapshot the exact state of a game so you can pick up right where you left off —
independent of the game's own in-cartridge saves.

- **Suspend points** capture the moment you stop, so closing and reopening a game resumes instantly.
- You can keep multiple save slots per game and switch between them.

Your normal in-game saves (battery/SRAM saves) keep working as usual, too.

## Rewind

Made a mistake? **Rewind** lets you roll the last few seconds of gameplay back and try again.

> Rewind is disabled while **hardcore** achievements mode is on — see **Achievements**.

## Screenshots & clips

You can capture a screenshot at any time, and grab a **clip** of what just happened with
"Clip that!". See **Screenshots & Clips**.

## Per-game options

Many settings — controller type, emulation options, and more — can be set globally or overridden
for a single game, so a title that needs something special keeps it without affecting the rest of
your library. See **Settings**.
