# Settings

Open [Settings](/settings) to configure Firelight. The settings are grouped into pages:

- **Appearance** — theme and look of the app.
- **Directories** — where your games, saves, and other files live. Start here to add your ROM
  folders.
- **Controllers** — gamepads, profiles, and button mapping (also on the
  [Controllers](/controllers) page).
- **Achievements** — sign in to RetroAchievements and toggle hardcore mode.
- **Audio / Video** — sound and display options.
- **Emulation** — how games run: frame pacing/sync, and other core behavior.
- **Platforms** — per-system options.
- **About** — version information.

## Global vs. per-game

Most emulation and controller settings have a **global** value that applies everywhere, and can be
**overridden for a single game**. This lets one demanding title use a special setting without
changing the rest of your library.

## Advanced settings

Some pages hide rarely-needed options behind an **advanced** toggle. Turn it on to reveal
lower-level controls (like individual core options); leave it off for a simpler view.

## Frame pacing

Under **Emulation** you can choose how Firelight paces frames (for example syncing to your monitor
or to audio). If a game feels stuttery or tears, this is the setting to experiment with.
