# Achievements

Firelight supports [RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org) — community-made challenges
that unlock as you play supported games.

## Signing in

Open [Settings](/settings) → **Achievements** and log in with your RetroAchievements account. Once
signed in, supported games show their achievement list, and you'll earn unlocks as you play.

If you don't have an account, create one for free at
[retroachievements.org](https://retroachievements.org).

## Earning achievements

While a supported game runs, Firelight tracks your progress and notifies you as achievements
unlock. You can review a game's achievement set — earned and unearned — from its details.

## Hardcore mode

**Hardcore** mode is the "no shortcuts" way to earn achievements and appear on leaderboards. When
it's on:

- **Rewind is disabled.**
- Save states and other assists are restricted.

Turn hardcore off if you'd rather play casually with rewind and save states; you'll still unlock
achievements, just not the hardcore versions.

## Offline play

You can keep earning while offline — unlocks sync to RetroAchievements the next time you're
connected and signed in.
