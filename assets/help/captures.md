# Screenshots & clips

Firelight can capture what's happening on screen while you play.

## Screenshots

Take a screenshot at any time during gameplay. Screenshots are saved to your screenshots folder
(see **Troubleshooting** for where files live). You can bind a screenshot shortcut on the
[Controllers](/controllers) page.

## Clips — "Clip that!"

Firelight keeps a short rolling buffer of recent gameplay, so you can save the last several seconds
**after** something cool happens — no need to start recording in advance. Trigger a clip and the
recent moment is written out as a video file.

## Finding your captures

Screenshots and clips live in your captures folders on disk. A built-in gallery for browsing them
inside the app is planned.

## Tips

- Clips are video-only by design (no audio track) in this version.
- If a capture shortcut isn't working, check that it's bound under
  [Controllers](/controllers) → Shortcuts.
