# Getting started

Welcome to **Firelight** — an emulation frontend that runs your retro games and keeps your
library organized, with achievements, save states, custom artwork, and more.

## The quick version

1. **Tell Firelight where your games are.** Open [Settings](/settings) → **Directories** and add
   the folder(s) that contain your ROMs. Firelight watches these folders and scans them
   automatically.
2. **Wait a moment.** New games appear in your [Library](/library) as they're found. Their real
   names, icons, and box art fill in automatically shortly after.
3. **Play.** Select a game to launch it.

That's the whole loop — add a folder, and your games show up ready to play.

## Finding your way around

The left sidebar is your main navigation:

- **Library** — all your games, with folders and filters.
- **Mod Shop** — browse and install mods and ROM hacks.
- **Controllers** — set up gamepads and button mappings.
- **Activity** — see what you've been playing.
- **Settings** — everything configurable.
- **Help** — you're here.
